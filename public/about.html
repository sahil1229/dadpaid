ABOUT

01What is this

A stupidly simple family travel expense tracker. Built for the people who go on trips together and then spend the flight home arguing about who paid for what.

It is not Splitwise, it is not corporate finance, it is not a budgeting app. It is a giant number pad, a couple of avatars, and an algorithm at the end that tells you who owes whom. Mild guilt, lightly tracked.

Do I need to be a dad?
No. The name is mostly aspirational. Use it with friends, partners, siblings, anyone. The settlement math doesn't check.
Is this just Splitwise?
No. Splitwise is for roommates arguing over rent. Dad Paid is for trips. One keypad, one ledger, one wrap screen at the end. Quieter UX.
Will my data be sold to advertisers?
No. We're not big enough to be evil yet. Also there is no us, this is a side project. See the privacy section below for the boring details.
PRIVACY

02What we collect, where it lives

Plain English. No legalese.

Email
Used as your sign-in identifier. Stored by our auth provider. Never shown to anyone outside your trips.
Your name & color
What you typed in onboarding. Shown only to people on your trips.
Expenses
Title, amount, currency, date, payer, category, split rule, and optional photo. Visible to everyone you've invited to that trip and nobody else.
Photos
Stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket. Each photo is scoped to your family. Only signed URLs are issued, valid for one hour.
Cards
Last 4 digits and nicknames only. We never see or store full card numbers, CVV, expiry, or any payment credential. Cards in this app are labels, not payment methods.
Cookies
One session cookie from our auth provider. No tracking cookies. No advertising cookies. No fingerprinting.
Analytics
None right now. If we add any, it will be opt-in and explicitly named here.

03Where it's stored

Supabase (Postgres database + auth + storage) runs on AWS. Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Vercel serves this site. Both are SOC 2 compliant.

We do not sell, license, or share your data with anyone except the services strictly required to make the app work. There are no third-party marketing or advertising integrations.

04Your rights

  • Export: Settings → DATA → EXPORT AS CSV. All your expenses, anytime.
  • Edit: Tap any row in the ledger to edit or delete. Tap any member or card in Settings.
  • Delete your account: email hello@dadpaid.app (placeholder address — will be wired up when we have one) and we'll wipe everything within 7 days. Your family + trips + expenses + photos cascade out cleanly.
  • Right to know: ask us what we have on you. The answer is "the email you signed up with, the rows you typed in, and the photos you uploaded." That's it.

05If you invite someone

When you generate an invite link and send it to a friend, that friend sees the trip you invited them to. They see every expense on that trip and every other guest. They don't see anything else you've ever created in Dad Paid. They join with their own email; you can revoke their access by signing out, deleting the trip, or removing them from settings.

TERMS

06The understanding

Use it for what it's for. Don't try to break it. We make no guarantees that the settlement math will hold up in court when your cousin refuses to pay for the Airbnb.

This is a side project. It's free. It may go down sometimes. If something matters financially, double-check it with whatever method you'd usually use. Don't sue us; we're a hobby.

If you find a bug, a leak, or anything weird, please tell us at hello@dadpaid.app and we will love you a normal amount.
